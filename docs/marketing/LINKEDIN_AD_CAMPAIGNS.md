## 3. Best Practices for Posting on LinkedIn

1. **First 2 Lines are Everything**: LinkedIn truncates posts after the second line with "...see more". The hook must create cognitive dissonance or address a burning pain point immediately.
2. **Never Put Links in the Main Post**: LinkedIn's algorithm penalizes posts containing external URLs because it wants to keep users on the platform. Always put your link in the **first comment** (e.g., *"🔗 Link to 30-day free trial in the comments below"*).
3. **Best Times to Post in East Africa (EAT)**:
   - **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday**: 7:30 AM – 8:30 AM EAT (Morning commute reading) or 12:30 PM – 1:30 PM EAT (Lunchtime peak).
4. **Engage in the Comments**: Respond to every comment within the first 60 minutes of posting to trigger LinkedIn's viral distribution algorithm.
