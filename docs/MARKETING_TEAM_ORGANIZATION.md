## 1. The Marketing Hook: Why Top Marketers Join Kabuor ERP

> *"Most marketing in African tech is frustrating: you spend thousands of dollars on Facebook and Google ads, only for users to bounce because the product doesn't fit local payment rails or power conditions. At Kabuor ERP, marketing is different: every business in Kenya is legally forced by KRA to become eTIMS compliant, and 84% of payments run on M-Pesa. We have built the exact product the market needs, and our onboarding takes under 3 minutes. Your job isn't to push a speculative tool; it's to distribute the financial operating system African commerce has been begging for."*

### The Narrative for Elite Candidates (Ex-Safaricom, Jumia, Twiga, Copia, Bolt):
1. **The Product Actually Works**: 127 automated tests passing, 500k daily tx capability, and a working offline PWA that never crashes during blackouts.
2. **Uncapped Upside**: Competitive base salaries in Kenya Shillings plus **10%–15% ESOP pool equity** tied to a high-growth Series A trajectory ($12M–$15M valuation).
3. **Multi-Channel Distribution Freedom**: Budget to run on-the-ground market blitzes, viral receipt attribution loops, and certified accountant affiliate networks.
