## 1. Executive Summary

**Kabuor ERP is the compound business operating system and financial ledger for East African commerce.** We replace fragmented, foreign software with a native platform that embeds real-time mobile money (M-Pesa), statutory tax compliance (KRA eTIMS), inventory, and automated payroll into a single operational core.

---

## 2. The Opportunity & Market Dynamics

* **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $4.2B across East Africa (over 1.5M formal and semi-formal SMEs).
* **The Regulatory Catalyst ("Why Now?")**:
  - The Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) has made **eTIMS compliance mandatory for all businesses**. Failure to issue cryptographic eTIMS receipts results in complete disqualification of business deductions and heavy fines.
  - Foreign ERPs (SAP, Odoo, Sage, NetSuite) **cannot natively comply** without expensive partner hardware ($500–$1,500/device) and weeks of consulting.
* **The Financial Rail Mismatch**:
  - 84% of commercial payments in Kenya settle via **Safaricom M-Pesa** (Paybills and Tills), yet imported accounting software forces manual export/import and daily reconciliations.

---

## 3. Product & Technology Moat

1. **Frictionless Onboarding (< 3 Minutes)**:
   - Self-serve onboarding wizard enables a non-technical shop owner to import products, issue their first KRA-signed tax invoice, and connect M-Pesa payments in under 180 seconds.
2. **Offline-First POS Kiosk**:
   - Progressive Web App (PWA) with native browser installation. Continues selling during power cuts or fiber outages from cached inventory; drains sales atomically upon reconnection.
3. **Double-Entry Financial Ledger**:
   - High-throughput modular monolith (.NET 10 Minimal APIs) benchmarked for **500,000+ daily transactions** with bank-grade sequence locking (`SequenceGate`) and multi-tenant isolation.
4. **Embedded Autonomous AI Agents**:
   - Built-in intelligent assistants that chase overdue receivables, draft statutory payroll, and match orphaned M-Pesa payments in English and Kiswahili.
